" collection: portfolio --- This project provides a simulation-based power analysis framework for heterogeneous treatment effect (HTE/CATE) estimation. It compares R-learner, DR-learner, and generalized random forest (GRF) estimators under controlled shifts to the data-generating process, using repeated-split best linear predictor (BLP) inference to assess statistical power. This work grew out of my role as a research assistant on heterogeneous treatment effects methodology at Duke University. **Repository:** [github.com/QingwenDeng0920/hte-power-analysis](https://github.com/QingwenDeng0920/hte-power-analysis) **Key features:** * Comparison of R-learner, DR-learner, and GRF estimators * Repeated-split BLP inference for power assessment * Written in R
