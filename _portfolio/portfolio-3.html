--- title: "Interference-Robust Causal Inference" excerpt: "An R framework for causal inference when SUTVA fails — spillover-robust difference-in-differences, double machine learning with spatial HAC standard errors, and near/far research designs, applicable to environmental policy, crime, and labor settings.
" collection: portfolio --- This project is an R framework for conducting causal inference when the stable unit treatment value assumption (SUTVA) fails due to spatial or social spillovers. It generalizes the identification strategies I developed for my working paper "Political Windows and the Dynamics of Temporary Pollution Abatement" — which relies on within-airshed ring designs and stacked difference-in-differences to separate genuine abatement from spatial displacement — into a reusable toolkit applicable beyond environmental policy, including crime and labor economics settings with plausible spillovers. **Repository:** [github.com/QingwenDeng0920/interference-robust-causal-inference](https://github.com/QingwenDeng0920/interference-robust-causal-inference) **Key features:** * Spillover-robust difference-in-differences estimators * Double machine learning with spatial HAC standard errors * Near/far research design implementation * Written in R
