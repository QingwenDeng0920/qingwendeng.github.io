I am a pre-doctoral research associate at Duke University's Nicholas School of the Environment, working with Professors Yichun Fan and Martin Smith. I hold a Master's in Political Economy from Duke University and a B.Econ from Renmin University of China.

My research sits at the intersection of political economy, environmental and resource economics, and social science methodology with a substantive focus on **institutional formation and evolution** : how institutions emerge, persist, and are selectively dismantled, particularly under authoritarian governance and in environmental policy contexts. I use tools spanning dynamic optimization, game theory, agent-based modeling, and quasi-experimental causal inference (Python, R, Stata, MATLAB).

I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027 entry in political science, applied/environmental economics, and related interdisciplinary fields. Feel free to reach out at [qingwen.deng@duke.edu](mailto:qingwen.deng@duke.edu).
